# Orchestral sample notice

The sustained and spiccato violin-section and cello-section samples in this directory are derived
from **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition** (VSCO 2 CE), recorded by
Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell and released under CC0 1.0 Universal:

- Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- License: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE

Chess Studio trims and encodes the source WAV recordings as MP3 for efficient
browser playback. The filenames retain the instrument, root note and source
variation; short articulations retain two round robins per pitch to avoid
repeating an identical bow attack. Attribution is not required by CC0, but is
preserved here in recognition of the original recording work.
