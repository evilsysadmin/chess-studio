## Invariants

- Board remains the visual and interaction focus.
- Curved observatory room; cream upper wall, deep green lower wall, dark wood/brass trim.
- Green/cream marble checkerboard floor plus a restrained circular green rug around the command table.
- Large round oculus: crescent moon and stars only. No orbital rings, connected constellation lines or mechanical spokes.
- Open ceiling. No crossing canopy rails/bars and no suspended armillary clutter.
- One cast-iron stove/fireplace on the left, aimed inward.
- Brass/teal telescope on the right.
- One premium leather reading chair and a compact shelf of chess treatises; props stay peripheral.
- Teal/copper entry door must physically meet a visible threshold/floor; a small rug may mark the entry.
- Warm practical lanterns with cooler moonlight; no dashboard-like decoration.

## Iteration rule

Every War Room v3 visual PR renders the deterministic Blender review PNG and compares it against this contract. CI being green is not visual acceptance; the generated PNG and the runtime capture must both preserve the composition.
