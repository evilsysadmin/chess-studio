# Pawn Slug · Combat feel

Tracking PR for the next focused gameplay-polish batch after P0 platforming.

## Scope
- Heavier impact feedback: hit-stop, localized flash, recoil/knockback and weapon-specific particles.
- Camera look-ahead, jump framing and boss-entry framing without nausea or mobile jitter.
- Enemy anticipation/recovery animation so pawn, knight, rook and bishop stop feeling robotic.
- Panzer-Rook three-phase fight driven by real HP thresholds: 100–65%, 65–30%, <30%.
- Phase-specific visual damage, smoke/sparks and restrained attack escalation.
- Preserve current damage/hitbox balance unless a change is explicitly justified by playability.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Add focused unit/browser coverage and iterate CI to green before ready-for-review.
