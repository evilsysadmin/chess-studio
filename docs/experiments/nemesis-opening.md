# Nemesis opening

## Goal
Identify openings that repeatedly hurt the player from real stored games and turn one into a focused, evidence-backed training rival.

## First slice
- Aggregate opening outcomes only when opening identity is actually known.
- Rank candidate nemeses using sample size, results and recurring tactical incidents.
- Surface one current nemesis with supporting evidence.
- Offer targeted training only when matching real positions/puzzles exist.
- Let improvement retire or demote a nemesis naturally.

## Guardrails
- No weakness claim below a minimum evidence threshold.
- No invented opening labels or tactical causes.
- Do not over-weight a single bad game.

## Acceptance
- Deterministic scoring and threshold tests.
- Existing `Así juegas` coaching remains evidence-only.
- Training CTA appears only with matching reconstructible material.
- Reset removes derived nemesis state predictably.
